# Hackathon Judging Strategy

## Technological implementation

Show that Qloo is used repeatedly and meaningfully:

- real entity resolution
- cross-domain Insights calls
- explainability requested
- location-aware place and destination discovery
- bounded retry and failure handling
- visible evidence trail

## Design

The product should feel like a premium cultural intelligence system, not a generic chatbot. The judge should be able to understand the brief, cultural signals, campaign idea and evidence without opening developer tools.

## Potential impact

The initial user is a marketer, agency, hotel, restaurant, destination or consumer brand that needs to understand cultural fit before spending on campaigns, partnerships or experiences.

## Quality of idea

The key differentiator is not recommendation. It is translation:

Taste relationships → cultural intersections → business opportunity.

## Demo story

Primary demo:

- Boutique hotel
- Guadalajara, Mexico
- Design-conscious American travelers ages 28-40
- Goal: increase direct bookings and cultural relevance
- Known interests: A24, Bad Bunny, Soho House

The demo should prove that Qloo signals change the strategy output.
